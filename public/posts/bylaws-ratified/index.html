Bylaws Ratified

February 11, 2025

The Democratic Socialists of Nanaimo ratified their bylaws last Wednesday the 5th of February, in an all-hands meeting. The newly-ratified bylaws can be found here and the meeting minutes can be found here.

In the meeting, an interim steering committee of 7 members was elected.
